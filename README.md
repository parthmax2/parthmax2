## About Me

I'm **Saksham Pathak**, a Generative AI Engineer and M.Sc. AI & ML graduate of IIIT Lucknow, building intelligent systems that can listen, reason, retrieve knowledge, and take action.

My work spans **Voice AI, Agentic Systems, RAG Pipelines, Fine-Tuning, and LLM Testing**, with a strong interest in real-time AI experiences that feel natural and human.

Currently, I'm exploring:

* 🎙️ Barge-in Voice Agents & Custom VAD
* 🧠 Agentic Workflows with Google ADK, LangGraph & MCP
* 🛡️ Agent Reliability: Failure Recovery, Guardrails & PII Redaction
* 🧪 Deterministic Testing & Evaluation for LLM Apps
* 🎭 Real-Time 3D Conversational Avatars

I enjoy turning ambitious AI ideas into practical systems that people can interact with, trust, and use every day.

## SYSTEMS

**`01` — ghostrun · Testing for LLM Apps**
pytest-native record/replay for LLM calls, semantic assertions via a local judge, and Bayesian prompt optimization. Offline, free, deterministic CI.
`Python` `pytest` `Ollama` · [![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/badge/pip%20install-1D4ED8?style=flat-square&logo=pypi&logoColor=white)](https://pypi.org/project/ghostrun/) [![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-1D4ED8?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/parthmax2/ghostrun)

**`02` — Saarthi AI · Commute-Planning Agent**
Autonomous Google ADK agent that sweeps future departure windows and fuses traffic, weather and local events into a transparent risk score, with persistent MongoDB MCP memory.
`Google ADK` `Gemini` `MongoDB MCP` `FastAPI` · [![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-1D4ED8?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/parthmax2/Saarthi-AI) [![Space](https://img.shields.io/badge/🤗%20Space-1D4ED8?style=flat-square)](https://huggingface.co/spaces/parthmax/SaarthiAI)

**`04` — DocuMind AI · Document Intelligence**
